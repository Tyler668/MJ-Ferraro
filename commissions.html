Process

How it works

You'll see progress photos along the way.

01

Share your photos

Send a few clear, well-lit photos and mention anything you'd like included, such as a favorite spot.

02

Choose a size

Pick a canvas size and a background: a solid color, a loose wash or a full scene.

03

Painting

Peg paints the portrait in acrylic and shares progress along the way.

04

Pick up or delivery

Pick up the finished portrait or have it delivered.

Pricing

Quoted piece by piece

Every portrait is priced on its own. What shapes the quote:

  • Canvas size, from a small square to a large statement piece
  • How many animals are in the portrait
  • The background: a solid colour, a loose wash or a full scene

Send a photo or two and Peg will come back with a price and a timeline.
