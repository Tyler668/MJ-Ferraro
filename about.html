About Peg

Peg paints Rhode Island harbors and landmarks, Gulf Coast dunes, marsh birds, garden flowers and seafood suppers.

She works mostly in acrylic, and also in oil, watercolor and mixed media. She paints on buoys, ornaments, bags, slate and furniture as well.

She also hosts Pasta & Paint parties, where groups paint along with her over dinner.
