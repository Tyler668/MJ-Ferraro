About the artist

M. J. Ferraro

Her artistic journey began on a whim, purchasing paints and canvas. Discovering an unknown talent, her passion ignited.

This has generated a creative path fueled by passion, nature, and love. M. J. hopes her paintings reflect this joy and wonder.

Her paintings have been exhibited in numerous galleries in Rhode Island and Massachusetts, and most recently in Alabama.
