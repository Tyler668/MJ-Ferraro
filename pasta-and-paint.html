FAQ

Questions

Do guests need painting experience?

No. The painting is broken into simple steps, with help for each guest along the way.

What will we paint?

Usually a coastal or seasonal scene, such as waves, a marsh at sunset or a lighthouse. The host can choose from a few options or request one.

Where do the parties happen?

In your home or at a hosted venue. A dining table and some floor space is enough.

What's included?

Canvases, paints, brushes, easels, aprons, the lesson and dinner.

What occasions is it good for?

Birthdays, showers, reunions, book clubs and team events.
